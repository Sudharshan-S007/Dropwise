Water-Saving Tips

Fix Leaks Promptly

A single dripping faucet can waste up to 3,000 gallons per year. Regular checks can save significant water.

Shorter Showers

Limit showers to 5 minutes to save up to 12.5 gallons per session. Use a timer to stay on track.

Efficient Appliances

Opt for water-efficient washing machines and dishwashers to cut down on usage significantly.
